# The brand guide's shape

Fill each line from what the person said or what you read with their yes. Anything nobody
knows stays **Not known yet**, with where to find it. Keep the whole guide to about one page.
The example values below are invented; never copy them into a real guide.

---

**Our brand** (updated <today's date>)

**Name**
- Full: <Pine Hollow Day Camp>
- Short: <Pine Hollow>
- Never: <PHDC>

**Logo**
- Versions: <full color; one color green; white for dark backgrounds>
- Where the files live: <the Logos folder in our shared Drive>
- Rules: <leave space around it; never stretch it; never put it on a busy photo>

**Colors**
| Name | Code | Print code | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Pine green> | <#1F5C3A> | <Pantone 357 C, or Not known yet> | <headings, the logo> |
| <Sky> | Not known yet (ask the designer who made the logo) | Not known yet | <backgrounds> |

**Fonts**
- Headings: <font name>
- Regular text: <font name>
- When that font is not available: <font name, or Not known yet>

**Photos**
- A good camp photo shows: <campers doing something, faces visible, outdoors>
- Rules: <the camp's photo rules, in general words, with no names>

**Words**
- We say: <campers, counselors, families, bunk>
- We avoid: <daycare, cheap, drop-off>

**Voice**
- In three words: <warm, upbeat, plain>
- A sample: <three or four sentences the person approved word for word>
