# The eleven words

Part of the get-started skill. Teach one word per message, in this order, in Ainsley's
voice. Say the word, then what it is in one plain sentence, then what it means for them in
one or two more. Use the camp example given, or a better one from their own job if Part 1 or
Part 5 told you about it. Handle each video slot the way the skill's "Video slots" section
says. These definitions match the glossary in explain-it-plainly; if they ever disagree,
that glossary is the one to fix, and this page follows it.

## 1. Harness

> Video slot, not made yet: "What a harness is"

The **harness** is the program built around the AI that gives it hands: it opens your
files, uses the apps you connected, follows written instructions and runs jobs on a
schedule. The app you are using right now is the harness. Chatting in a plain window mostly
talks about work; a harness can do some of it. When something "can't find your file", the
harness usually is not connected to that place yet, which is a different fix from a wrong
answer.

## 2. Model

> Video slot, not made yet: "What a model is"

The **model** is the AI itself, the part that reads what you typed and writes back. Apps
offer several, from the most careful to the fastest, and the one already picked is fine for
nearly everything. If they ever move to another AI app, the model is one of the two things
that changes; their notes and this plugin come with them.

## 3. Usage

> Video slot, not made yet: "What usage means"

**Usage** is how much of your plan's allowance you have used. Paid plans let you do a lot,
with a limit that resets after a while. A long job, a big attachment or a very long
conversation uses more. If they hit the limit, the app says so, and the work waits until it
resets. Where the app shows usage depends on the app; offer to find it with them on screen,
and never describe that screen from memory.

## 4. Tokens

> Video slot, not made yet: "What tokens are"

**Tokens** are the small pieces of words the model reads and writes in, often a short word
or part of a longer one. Usage is counted in tokens. A forty-page handbook is a lot of
tokens; a three-line question is a few. Nobody needs to count them. It is only the reason a
huge file uses more of the allowance than a short note.

## 5. Plugin

> Video slot, not made yet: "What a plugin is"

A **plugin** is a bundle of skills and rules you add to the app. This one is made for
people who run summer camps, and it is why the app here never sends anything and knows to
look in their notes. When it updates, the new version arrives on its own.

## 6. Skill

> Video slot, not made yet: "What a skill is"

A **skill** is a written page of instructions the AI follows for one job, like the
laminated instructions taped inside the arts and crafts cabinet. Plain sentences, not code.
"Wrap up" is a skill; so is the one running right now. They can make their own: at the end
of a job they do every week, they type "make this a skill". To change one that came with
the plugin, they ask for "make my own copy", because updates replace the originals.

## 7. Context

> Video slot, not made yet: "What context is"

**Context** is everything the AI can see right now, in this conversation: what they typed,
what they attached and what it read. It forgets all of it when the conversation ends, which
is why their Personal Notes exist. A very long conversation gets crowded, so a new job goes
best in a new conversation.

## 8. Wiki

> Video slot, not made yet: "What a wiki is"

A **wiki** is a set of pages a group of people can add to and correct over time. They have
three layers: their Personal Notes, their Camp Wiki and the CG Knowledge Base (Part 5 of the
skill explains them). Every answer that comes from a wiki says which page it came from, so
they can check it.

## 9. Prompt

> Video slot, not made yet: "What a prompt is"

A **prompt** is just the thing they type to ask for something. A good one says who it is
for, what they want and what would make it right. Show this pair:

> Weak: "Write something about the bus."
>
> Better: "Write a four-sentence note to families on the Maple Ridge bus saying Tuesday's
> bus runs twenty minutes late all week."

## 10. Goal

> Video slot, not made yet: "What a goal is"

A **goal** is a whole list of work handed over at once, with what "done" looks like for
each item, for example "check every one of these forty applications for a missing
reference". The AI works through it one item at a time and ticks each off. They type "build
a goal" to make one.

## 11. Loop

> Video slot, not made yet: "What a loop is"

A **loop** is a job that goes round: do one piece, check it, then the next, until the list
is done or a stopping point is reached. A goal runs as a loop. Inbox helper is a loop on a
timer: every few hours it reads new email, drafts the replies its notes can answer, and
lists the rest.
